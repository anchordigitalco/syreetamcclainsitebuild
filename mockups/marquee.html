Where Leadership Meets Legacy

Dr. Syreeta McClain is an accomplished educational leader, high school principal, consultant, speaker, and nonprofit co-founder with more than two decades of experience transforming the lives of young people and the systems that serve them. She specializes in customized high school programming, alternative education, student engagement, leadership development, and creating innovative pathways for student success. As the CEO of Premier Leadership, LLC, she provides leadership coaching, professional development, workshops, program development, and keynote presentations for educators, schools, organizations, parents, and community leaders.

She is also the co-founder of the Everyday Legends Foundation, a nonprofit committed to strengthening communities grounded in their three pillars: education, athletics, and service. Drawing from her unique experience as an educator, executive leader, and nonprofit founder, Dr. McClain now bridges her skillsets with the evolving landscape of sports and NIL, as athletic manager to her sons, while empowering individuals and families to maximize their impact.
